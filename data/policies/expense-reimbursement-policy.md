# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

*Sample policy written for the DISHA HR demo. Replace with your company's approved policy before real use.*
Owner: Finance · Version 1.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## 1. What can be claimed

| Category | Covers | Guideline limit |
|---|---|---|
| Travel | Cabs, trains, flights and hotels for company work | Economy fare; hotels up to ₹6,000 per night |
| Meals | Client meals and team meals during work | ₹1,000 per person per meal |
| Internet | Home broadband for employees working remotely | ₹1,200 per month |
| Training | Courses, certifications and conference fees | ₹15,000 per year, manager approval first |
| Other | Office supplies and anything not listed above | ₹3,000 per claim |

Amounts above these guidelines need written approval from the manager **before** the spend, attached to the claim.

## 2. What cannot be claimed

Personal travel, alcohol, fines and traffic penalties, personal subscriptions and entertainment, gifts to
clients without prior approval, and any expense already paid directly by the company.

## 3. How to claim

1. Open the **Expenses** page in the DISHA HR app and press New claim.
2. Enter the date of spend, category, amount in rupees and a short description.
3. Attach the receipt. Files up to 7 MB are accepted. Claims above ₹500 without a receipt are rejected.
4. Submit. The claim goes to the employee's reporting manager; employees reporting to an admin are approved by Finance.

Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the spend. Older claims need an explanation and HR approval.

## 4. Approval and payment

- Managers approve or reject their team's claims on the Expenses page. Employees can cancel their own claim while pending.
- Rejected claims should be corrected and submitted again, not resubmitted unchanged.
- Approved claims are paid with the **next monthly salary**, and appear as reimbursement, not as salary.
- Reimbursements are not taxable income, so they are not part of gross pay on the payslip.

## 5. Who can see what

Employees see their own claims. Managers see their own and their team's. Finance and HR administrators see all
claims and can filter by employee. Receipts follow the same rule: a colleague can never open another
colleague's receipt.

## 6. Travel booking

Book travel through the office administrator wherever possible, so the company is billed directly and no
claim is needed. Only book personally when the administrator cannot arrange it in time.
