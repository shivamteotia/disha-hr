# Payroll and Payslips Policy

*Sample policy written for the DISHA HR demo. Replace with your company's approved policy before real use.*
Owner: Finance · Version 1.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## 1. Pay cycle

- Salary is paid **monthly**, on or before the last working day of the month.
- The pay period is the first to the last day of the calendar month.
- Payment is by bank transfer to the account registered with HR. Tell HR at least ten days before month end
  if the account changes.

## 2. What makes up the salary

Each payslip shows these components:

| Component | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Basic | The base salary component |
| HRA | House Rent Allowance, 40% of basic |
| Allowances | Other allowances; may include an annual performance bonus in March |
| **Gross** | Basic + HRA + Allowances |
| Deductions | Provident Fund at 12% of basic, plus professional tax |
| **Net pay** | Gross minus deductions, the amount credited to the bank |

## 3. Deductions explained

- **Provident Fund (PF):** 12% of basic, matched by the company, deposited to the employee's PF account.
- **Professional Tax (PT):** a fixed state levy, currently ₹200 per month.
- **Income tax (TDS):** not yet deducted through this system. Employees are responsible for their own advance
  tax until payroll tax calculation goes live. HR will announce the change.
- **Leave without pay:** each LWP day reduces gross pay by one day's salary, calculated on the month's working days.

## 4. Viewing and downloading payslips

Payslips are on the **Payslips** page of the DISHA HR app. Open any month and use Print to save it as a PDF
for a visa, loan or rental application.

**Employees can only ever see their own payslips.** Managers cannot see their team's salary. Only HR and
Finance administrators can see everyone's, and only they can create or correct a payslip. Disha will refuse to
disclose another employee's salary, whoever asks.

## 5. Corrections

Report any error in a payslip to Finance within **30 days** of release. Corrections appear in the next
month's payslip along with an explanation.

## 6. Reimbursements

Approved expense claims are paid together with salary but are kept separate from it, and are not part of gross
pay. See the Expense and Reimbursement Policy.

## 7. Confidentiality

Salary details are confidential. Sharing your own or anyone else's salary information outside the company, or
discussing it in a way that identifies a colleague, is a breach of the Code of Conduct.

## 8. Questions

Ask Disha in the app, or write to finance@company.com.
