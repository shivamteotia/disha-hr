# Remote Work and IT Security Policy

*Sample policy written for the DISHA HR demo. Replace with your company's approved policy before real use.*
Owner: IT and HR · Version 1.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## 1. Hybrid working

- The default is **three days in the office** (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) and up to two days remote.
- Teams may agree different days with their manager, as long as the team has enough overlap.
- Fully remote arrangements need HR approval and are reviewed every six months.
- Remote days still require check-in and check-out in the DISHA HR app, and being reachable during working hours.

## 2. Working from another city or country

Tell HR at least two weeks before working from another city for more than a week, since it can affect tax and
insurance. Working from outside India needs written HR approval before travel.

## 3. Equipment

The company provides a laptop and, on request, a monitor and headset. Home broadband is reimbursable up to
₹1,200 per month; see the Expense and Reimbursement Policy. Report loss, theft or damage to IT the same day.

## 4. Accounts and passwords

- Use your company account for work. Do not use personal email for company work.
- Passwords must be at least eight characters and unique to each service.
- Never share a password or a login link, including with colleagues and IT staff. IT will never ask for your password.
- Lock your screen when you step away.
- Report a suspected compromise immediately to it@company.com.

## 5. Handling data

- Keep employee, salary and client data inside approved company systems.
- Do not upload company or employee data to personal cloud storage or to public AI chatbots.
- Disha, inside the HR app, is approved for HR questions: it only shows each person the data they are already
  allowed to see.
- Share documents by link with named colleagues, not by public link.
- Print employee data only when necessary, and shred it after use.

## 6. Devices and networks

- Keep the operating system and browser updated, and leave disk encryption and antivirus switched on.
- Avoid public Wi-Fi for company work; use a personal hotspot or the company VPN instead.
- Do not install unlicensed software on company devices.

## 7. Phishing

Treat unexpected attachments and payment or credential requests with suspicion, even when they appear to come
from a senior colleague. Verify by phone or in person, and forward suspicious email to it@company.com.

## 8. Leaving the company

Access is withdrawn on the last working day, and all equipment must be returned. Copying company data before
leaving is a breach of the Code of Conduct.

## 9. Questions

Ask Disha in the app, or write to it@company.com.
