# Leave Policy

*Sample policy written for the DISHA HR demo. Replace with your company's approved policy before real use.*
Owner: HR · Version 1.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## 1. Leave types and yearly entitlement

Every confirmed employee receives the following leave each calendar year (1 January to 31 December):

| Code | Leave type | Days per year | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| CL | Casual Leave | 12 | Short personal matters, family events, errands |
| SL | Sick Leave | 12 | Illness, medical appointments, recovery |
| EL | Earned Leave | 15 | Planned holidays and longer breaks |
| LWP | Leave Without Pay | No limit | Used when paid leave is exhausted; salary is deducted pro rata |

Employees who join mid-year receive the full entitlement for that year. The company does not currently
pro-rate entitlement by joining date.

## 2. How leave days are counted

Leave is counted in **working days only**. Saturdays, Sundays and company holidays that fall inside a leave
period are not deducted from the balance. For example, leave applied from Friday to Monday where that Friday
is a company holiday counts as **one day**.

Half-day leave is not supported in the current system. Apply for a full day.

## 3. Applying for leave

1. Apply on the **Leaves** page of the DISHA HR app, choosing the leave type, start date, end date and a reason.
2. The request goes to the employee's **reporting manager**. Employees who report directly to an admin are
   approved by HR.
3. The manager approves or rejects it on the same page. Employees can cancel their own request while it is
   still pending.
4. Applications that exceed the remaining balance for that leave type are rejected by the system. Apply LWP instead.
5. Overlapping leave requests are not allowed. Cancel the existing request first.
6. Leave requests cannot span a year end. Split them into two requests.

## 4. Notice expected

| Leave | Notice |
|---|---|
| Earned Leave (EL) | At least 7 calendar days before the first day |
| Casual Leave (CL) | At least 1 working day before, wherever possible |
| Sick Leave (SL) | Inform the manager on the first day of absence; apply in the app on return |

Sick leave of **three or more consecutive days** requires a medical certificate to be uploaded on the
**Documents** page.

## 5. Balances, carry-forward and encashment

- Balances shown in the app count both approved and pending requests, so a pending request already holds the days.
- Unused CL and SL **lapse** on 31 December.
- Unused EL does not currently carry forward in the system. HR reviews exceptions case by case.
- Leave encashment is not offered at present.

## 6. Public holidays

The company publishes its holiday list for the year on the **Leaves** page. Holidays are fixed by HR and do
not consume leave balance.

## 7. Unapproved absence

Absence without an approved leave request is treated as LWP and may be raised by the manager with HR.
Repeated unapproved absence is handled under the disciplinary process in the Code of Conduct.
