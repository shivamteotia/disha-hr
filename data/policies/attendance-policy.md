# Attendance and Working Hours Policy

*Sample policy written for the DISHA HR demo. Replace with your company's approved policy before real use.*
Owner: HR · Version 1.0 · Effective 1 January 2026

## 1. Working days and hours

- The working week is **Monday to Friday**. Saturdays, Sundays and published company holidays are non-working days.
- Standard working hours are **9:30 AM to 6:30 PM**, including a one-hour lunch break, totalling **8 working hours** per day.
- A flexible start between 8:30 AM and 10:15 AM is allowed with the manager's agreement, as long as the full
  eight hours are completed.

## 2. Marking attendance

Attendance is recorded in the DISHA HR app:

1. Press **Check in** on the Dashboard when starting work.
2. Press **Check out** when finishing.
3. Only one check-in and one check-out are recorded per day. The app shows the hours worked once both exist.

Check-in times are recorded in the server's time zone. A day with no check-in and no approved leave counts as
an unexplained absence.

## 3. Forgotten or wrong entries (regularisation)

If a check-in or check-out is missing or wrong, ask HR to correct it. HR administrators can set or fix any
employee's attendance for a date on the **Attendance** page. Employees cannot edit their own attendance records.
Raise corrections within **7 days** of the date concerned.

## 4. Late arrival and short hours

- Arriving after 10:15 AM without informing the manager is treated as a late mark.
- Three or more late marks in a calendar month are discussed by the manager with the employee.
- Persistent short hours, meaning under six hours on several days without an approved reason, are handled under
  the Code of Conduct.

## 5. Overtime

The current system does not record overtime or shift allowances. Employees asked to work on a holiday or
weekend should agree compensatory time off with their manager and apply for it as Casual Leave, noting the
reason in the application.

## 6. Attendance and payroll

Attendance itself does not change salary. Unpaid leave (LWP) does: each LWP day reduces that month's pay pro
rata, as described in the Payroll and Payslips policy.

## 7. Viewing records

- **Employees** see their own attendance month by month on the Attendance page.
- **HR administrators** see everyone's records and can filter by employee.
- **Managers** can see their team's leave and expense requests, but not their team's daily attendance. Ask HR
  if you need a team attendance report.
